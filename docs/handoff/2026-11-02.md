## Loose ends and ideas
- The color roles come from `scripts/m3-tokens.mjs`, which follows the M3 tone table in CIE L*. Google's
  `@material/material-color-utilities` (HCT) wasn't installable in that session; swapping it in would change hues and
  chroma slightly, not the contrast between roles.
- The level-up seal was switched to the fixed gold role for dark mode at the end; it wasn't rechecked on screen.
- Main bundle is about 71 kB gzipped; `src/components/m3/icons.js` holds only the icons in use (`scripts/icons.txt`).
- Regenerating the font subset (`public/fonts/GoogleSansFlex-latin.woff2`) from `ofl/googlesansflex` in google/fonts:
  `fonttools varLib.instancer GoogleSansFlex[ROND,opsz,slnt,wdth,wght].ttf slnt=0 wdth=100 wght=300:900 opsz=18 ROND=0:100`,
  then `pyftsubset` to Latin with `--flavor=woff2` (needs the `brotli` module).
